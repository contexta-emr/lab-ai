Contexta — Top 10 Laboratory Panels

Canonical panel and analyte reference for Document Intelligence / Lab Result Extraction

Purpose: This is a proposed Contexta canonical vocabulary for the first 10 high-frequency laboratory panel groups. The codes below are Contexta internal canonical codes, not claimed to be universal industry-standard laboratory codes. Where appropriate, these can later be mapped to external standards such as LOINC.
